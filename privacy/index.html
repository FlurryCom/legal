Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Who this applies to

FlurryCom Postiz is a self-hosted scheduling tool used by its owner for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from other TikTok users.

2. What we receive from TikTok

When the owner connects their TikTok account through Login Kit, the app receives, with the permissions the owner approves: the account's open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic); profile link and bio (user.info.profile); follower, like, following and video counts (user.info.stats); and the list of the owner's public videos (video.list). It also receives the access and refresh tokens needed to act on the owner's account.

3. How it is used

The data is used only to show the connected account in the scheduler, to publish the owner's own videos to that account (video.upload and video.publish, through the Content Posting API), and to show basic performance numbers to the owner.

4. Storage and sharing

Data is stored in the tool's own database, on infrastructure controlled by its owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens and account data are kept only while the account is connected. Disconnecting the account in the tool removes access, and the owner can also revoke the app in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. To request deletion of any stored data, open an issue at the contact below.

Política de Privacidade (resumo em português)

O FlurryCom Postiz é uma ferramenta própria, não pública. Ao conectar o TikTok, o app recebe os dados do perfil do próprio dono (nome, foto, bio, contagens e vídeos públicos) e os tokens de acesso, usados só para mostrar a conta, publicar os vídeos do dono e exibir métricas básicas. Os dados ficam no banco da própria ferramenta, não são vendidos nem compartilhados. Ao desconectar a conta ou revogar o app no TikTok, o acesso é removido.
